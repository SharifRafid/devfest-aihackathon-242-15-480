# Synthetic Data Assumptions

All data in `data/out/` is generated by `data/generate.py` with a fixed seed. **No production, scraped, or real personal data is used anywhere.** Identifiers are random hex strings. Every number below is an assumption chosen to produce realistic *patterns*, not realistic *values*.

## Scale
- 3,000 users, 30 days (September 2026), ≈ 20k sessions, ≈ 300k events.
- 20% of **users** (not sessions) held out as the test set, so per-user baselines can never leak from train to test.

## Event vocabulary
- `s_*` events are screens rendered by the genuine app (splash, login, otp, home, balance, send, recipient, amount, confirm, pin, success, cashout, recharge, bill, qr, addmoney, statement, profile, help, pinchange, beneficiary).
- `a_*` events are backend API calls. `a_ping`, `a_offers`, `a_heartbeat` are **telemetry** the genuine app always emits (analytics ping on splash/home/success, offers prefetch on home, heartbeat every 30 s).
- Money-moving APIs: `a_transfer`, `a_cashout`, `a_recharge`, `a_billpay`, `a_merchantpay`.

## Human personas (weights)
| Persona | Weight | Dwell × | ms/char | PIN-error p | Usual hour (mean ± sd) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| student | 22% | 0.75 | 180 | 4% | 20 ± 3 | recharge/merchant heavy |
| salaried | 25% | 1.00 | 230 | 5% | 19 ± 3.5 | bills, send money |
| rmg_worker | 18% | 1.25 | 320 | 8% | 20 ± 2.5 | send-home + cash-out heavy |
| small_merchant | 12% | 1.00 | 240 | 5% | 14 ± 5 | many sessions/day, balance checks |
| elderly | 10% | 1.90 | 520 | 14% | 11 ± 3 | slow, back-navigation 22% |
| rural_lowlit | 13% | 1.60 | 450 | 12% | 12 ± 4 | 50% feature-phone, single device |

Screen dwell = lognormal(base, σ) × persona multiplier × per-user speed (N(1, 0.15)); typing screens add chars × ms/char. API latency lognormal(350 ms, 0.45). Daily send-money cap assumed ৳25,000. 8% of human transfers go to a never-seen recipient. 3% of human sessions are from a new device.

**Why the slow personas matter:** the fairness test checks that elderly and low-literacy users are not flagged merely for being slow.

## Injected attack archetypes (session prevalence)
| Code | Archetype | Prevalence | Injected pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Scripted ATO via reverse-engineered API | 0.8% | Only `a_*` events, no screens, no telemetry; uniform 90–320 ms gaps; 1–4 transfers to new recipients, 30–100% of cap; 80% new device; any hour |
| A2 | OTP-relay / SIM-swap takeover | 0.4% | New device; fast typing regardless of victim persona; PIN change → add beneficiary → 80–100% cap transfer; often cash-out |
| A3 | Coerced victim (scam call) | 0.6% | Victim's own device and rhythm; first-ever recipient; 60–100% of cap; 2–4× hesitation on amount/confirm; 55% at least one PIN error; 30% at night |
| A5 | Gambling / fake-purchase laundering | 0.4% | 22:00–05:00; 3–6 merchant payments of round amounts to 2 new merchants; often followed by cash-out |
| A6 | Emulator farm | 0.5% | 12 shared device ids across accounts; farm-wide fixed dwell per screen with 3% variance; ৳480–500 transfers |
| A7 | Adaptive scripted attacker | 0.5% | Same as A1 but with human-like lognormal(2.5 s, 0.6) gaps — tests robustness to timing mimicry |
| A4 | Personal wallet acting as merchant/agent | 2% of **users** | Account-level: 150–600 inbound tx/30d from 60–95% unique senders, 20–45 cash-outs, 60–90% round amounts |

Overall fraud session rate ≈ 3.2%, deliberately imbalanced.

## Known simplifications
- No real geolocation; region is a categorical user attribute.
- Inbound transactions are aggregated at account level only (not as session events).
- Scam-call audio/content is never modeled; only its behavioral footprint.
- Attack prevalence is far higher than reality to give the models enough positives; the README reports metrics at a realistic base rate too.
